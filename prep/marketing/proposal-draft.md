## 1. Our read of the problem

BFT costs RM520 a month. Prospects don't know the brand, compare it with a RM180 budget gym,
and see aesthetic posts and a 50% offer. Leads come in, about 10% convert, and your sales
team can only handle 6–7 a day. So **more leads is not the answer. Better-informed leads
are.**

Our approach in one line: **show people what happens inside a BFT class before they ever
message you**, so the leads that arrive already understand the system and want it.

We also know your business from the inside. We have already worked on Coach Hub, your coach
scheduling and pay system, so we understand your studios, programs and growth plans
(Subang, Bukit Jalil).

## 2. Content pillars (education first, entertaining always)

| Pillar | What we show | Example pieces |
|---|---|---|
| **The tech** | Your name on the screen, heart-rate zones, video demo at every station, no chalkboard | "What happens when you walk into BFT", POV first class, "the screen that knows your name" |

| **Global** | 330 studios, 70,000 members, one membership worldwide | "Same workout in Singapore this week", travelling-member stories |

**Mix per month:** about 50% educational, 25% entertaining, 15% community/proof, 10%
conversion-focused. Coaches' own content runs alongside. We give them simple monthly
prompts so it fits the same pillars, rather than replacing it.

**Tone:** fun, welcoming, tech-enabled, never "atas". Reference BFT Singapore/Australia
for feel, rewritten for a Malaysian audience.

## 3. Paid media and lead generation

**Current setup review (first 2 weeks):** audit targeting, ad sets, funnel and the WhatsApp
handoff. Find where the unready leads come from.

**Proposed funnel:**

1. **Educate (cold audience):** short videos from the pillars above, location-targeted
   around Bangsar (and later Subang / Bukit Jalil), middle to upper-middle income adults.
2. **Retarget (people who watched 50%+):** proof and value: member stories, PXI, price per
   class.
3. **Convert:** click to WhatsApp with 2–3 qualifying questions (goal, area, when they can
   train) before booking. Your sales team gets fewer, warmer conversations.

**Offer strategy (your question on the 50% two-week trial):** run them side by side for 4
weeks after 11.11. Split the budget: the current 50% trial ad vs an **education-first ad
that leads to a booked first class**. Compare **trial-to-member conversion**, not cost per
lead. Keep whichever brings members, not freebie-seekers.

**Leads beyond sales capacity:** we cap daily volume by budget pacing, so leads don't
arrive faster than your team can call them. Optional: an AI first reply on WhatsApp (see
section 6).

**Ad account and billing** stay on BFT's account and card. We manage targeting, creative
and A/B tests. Access via HQ or a shared account; we'll plan time for HQ approval.
