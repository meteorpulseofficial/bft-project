# BFT Bangsar: Meta campaign plan for boutique spenders (draft, 23 Nov 2026)

**Who:** people aged 32–45 around Bangsar who already pay for fitness: pilates (reformer or mat),
spin, HIIT/bootcamp studios, a personal trainer, ClassPass, or run clubs.
**Why them first:** they already spend RM300–800 a month, so at RM130 a week BFT looks *cheaper*,
not dearer. They're used to classes, so there's less fear to overcome.
**The one message:** *You already pay boutique prices. You're just not getting boutique results.*

Facts to confirm with the studio before launch are marked **(confirm)**.

---

## 1. Campaign structure

```
META_Conv_BoutiqueSpenders_WhatsApp_2026-12        (campaign budget split by Meta, CBO)
├── Ad set A — Broad (≈70% of budget)
│     7–8 km around Jalan Telawi · age 30–48 · all genders · NO interests
│     12 ads: 2 master ads × 6 identities (section 3) + 4 statics (section 5)
├── Ad set B — Lookalike test (≈15%)
│     1% lookalike of members who stayed 3+ months (Glofox export) · same location
│     best 4 ads from ad set A, copied in after week 1
└── Ad set C — "Zombie" (from week 3)
      ads Meta starved of spend that we still believe in, given a second chance

META_Retarget_BoutiqueSpenders_2026-12             (≈15% of budget)
└── Watched 50%+ of a video / engaged on IG or FB in 30 days / clicked WhatsApp but didn't book
      4 ad types (section 6)
```

**Exclude everywhere:** current members (customer list), leads from the last 30 days.
**Goal setting:** WhatsApp conversations started (or leads, if using a form). Keep the price in the
ad so the people who message already accept it.
**Budget:** RM4–5k a month to start; raise by 20% at a time, every 3–5 days, only when cost per
qualified lead holds.

## 3. Master ads + identity swaps (the "one-keyword" method)

each version to the people it describes.

## 5. Statics (cheap, native, make 3–5 new ones a week)

1. **Receipt:** "Last month: Pilates 8 × RM85, Spin 4 × RM65 = RM940. BFT: unlimited, RM520."
   *(use realistic, not exaggerated, numbers)*
2. **Notes-app screenshot:** "Things I stopped doing after switching: booking 3 apps · guessing my
   workout · paying RM80 a class · wondering if it's working."
3. **WhatsApp chat:** friend: "you still doing reformer?" → "switched lol, look" + photo of the
   screen with names and medals.
4. **Phone photo of the studio screen** with medals; caption "Gold. First time this month."
5. **Two columns:** "Your class: one instructor, nothing recorded. BFT: 2–3 coaches, every rep
   scored." **(confirm coaches)**
6. **App screenshot:** a month of logged workouts and medals; caption "This is what 'progress' looks
   like when someone's actually counting."
7. **Question card:** "When did your class last tell you your heart rate?"
8. **Calendar screenshot:** classes from 6am to 8pm; caption "No more 7am booking wars." **(confirm
   classes rarely fill)**

## 6. Retargeting (people who watched or clicked but didn't book)

Run all four at once, to the same audience:

| Type | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Objection** | "RM130 a week sounds like a lot — until you divide it by the classes you'll take." / "Already doing pilates? Keep it. Add the strength and cardio it skips." |
| **Proof carousel** | 4–5 slides: real members (job + what they did before + how often they come now) and a screenshot of their app progress |
| **Different offer** | Bring-a-friend class, or a BFT Unlock / open day invitation, instead of the trial again |
| **Value first** | "Free heart-rate check: find your real training zones in one class." Useful even if they don't join. **(needs studio OK)** |

Get the objection lines from real conversations: call 10 leads who didn't join and ask why.

## 7. After the click: WhatsApp and landing page

- **Pre-filled WhatsApp message per ad** (e.g. "Hi, I saw the pilates maths ad") so every lead is
  traced to its identity and angle.
- **First reply:** ask what they train now, which area, best time. Mention the price again before
  booking: anyone still keen is a real lead.
- **Landing page (Squarespace):** once a headline wins on Meta, copy it word for word as the page
  headline. Matching the ad's promise lifts page conversion noticeably.
- Record the lead's source in Glofox so we can see which identity becomes members, not just leads.

---

## 8. Test plan and decision rules

**Weeks 1–2:** ad set A only. 12 ads. Let Meta spread the budget.
**Weeks 3–4:** move winners into the lookalike set; start retargeting; launch the zombie set; add
3–5 new statics a week.
**Week 5+:** scale winners 20% every 3–5 days; keep at least 3 tests running (an ad, the page, the
offer).

| Measure (weekly) | Why |
|---|---|
| Cost per **qualified** lead (knows price, in area, books a trial) | the real lead cost |
| Trial booked % and show-up % | are they serious |
| **Trial → member %** | the number that matters most |
| Cost per new member | what a member really costs |
| Leads per day vs the 6–7 cap | never flood sales |

**Rules of thumb (set the real numbers with the studio):**
- Pause an ad that has spent twice the target cost per qualified lead without one qualified lead.
- An identity whose leads book trials but rarely join: fix the message after the click, not the ad.
- If leads go over the sales cap, make the filter stronger (price line first, smaller radius).
