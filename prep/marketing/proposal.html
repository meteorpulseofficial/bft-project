Marketing proposal: BFT Bangsar

Prepared for Studio 613 Sdn Bhd · by Hiiiilo · 18 November 2026

In one line: show people what happens inside a BFT class before they message you, so the leads that arrive already understand the system and want it.

1. Our read of the problem

BFT costs RM520 a month. Most prospects don't know the brand, compare it with a RM180 budget gym, and see aesthetic posts and a 50% offer. Leads come in, about 10% convert, and your sales team can handle only 6–7 a day.

So more leads is not the answer. Better-informed leads are. We'll measure success by members who join, not by likes or cost per lead.

Why us: we already work inside your business on Coach Hub, your coach scheduling and pay system. We know your studios, programs, coaches and growth plans (Subang Jaya, Bukit Jalil). A new agency starts from zero.

3. Sample concepts for BFT MADE FOR THIS PITCH

Three short-form video ideas, written for BFT Bangsar to show how we'd bring the pillars to life.

① "The gym already knew my name": Reel / TikTok, 30–40 sec · Pillar: the tech
  • Hook (0–3s): POV walking in. Text on screen: "I walked into a gym and it already knew my name."
  • Middle: name appears on the studio screen → station number → the video demo at the station → heart-rate tile turns from blue to red mid-workout.
  • Payoff: end of class, the medal appears. "No chalkboard. No guessing. Just show up."
  • Ad use: cold audience, Bangsar radius. Click to WhatsApp: "Curious how it works?"
② "Is RM520 a month expensive? Let's do the maths.": talking-head + B-roll, 45 sec · Pillar: value
  • Hook: a coach holds up a calculator. "RM520 for a gym? Let's do the maths."
  • Middle: unlimited classes, all day. Train 5 days a week and it's about RM26 a class, with a coach, heart-rate tracking and a new program every week.
  • Payoff: "Not the cheapest gym. The one you'll actually keep going to."
  • Ad use: retarget people who watched concept ① or visited the website.
③ "Can a total beginner get gold?": challenge series, 60 sec · Pillar: PXI and medals
  • Hook: "First BFT class ever. Can I get a gold medal?"
  • Middle: a coach explains in one line that medals are based on your own heart-rate target, not on being the fittest person in the room. Cuts of the first-timer working hard.
  • Payoff: the medal reveal, plus the member's honest reaction. Repeatable weekly with new first-timers, members' friends or creators.
  • Ad use: mid-funnel. Takes away the "I'm not fit enough" fear that stops many prospects.

4. Paid media and lead generation

Weeks 1–2: review what's running now. Targeting, ad sets, funnel and the WhatsApp handoff. Find where the unready leads come from.

Proposed funnel

  1. Educate (cold audience): short pillar videos, location-targeted around Bangsar (later Subang Jaya and Bukit Jalil), middle to upper-middle income adults.
  2. Retarget (watched 50%+): proof and value: member stories, PXI, price per class.
  3. Convert: click to WhatsApp with 2–3 short qualifying questions (goal, area, when they can train) before booking, so your sales team talks to warmer people.

Your question: the 50% two-week trial

Run both side by side for 4 weeks after 11.11: the current 50% trial ad vs an education-first ad that leads to a booked first class. Judge them on trial-to-member conversion, not cost per lead. Keep whichever brings members, not freebie-seekers.

Leads beyond sales capacity

We pace the ad budget so leads don't arrive faster than your team can follow up (about 6–7 a day). Optional: an AI first reply on WhatsApp (see section 7).

Ad account and billing stay on BFT's account and card. We manage targeting, creative and A/B tests, and plan for HQ approval on account access.
