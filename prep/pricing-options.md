not quotes.

## The idea: profit share based on members joining

**Recommendation: not for Coach Hub.** Partnership is a good instinct, but tie it to
something this app actually moves.

Why it doesn't fit:

1. **Coach Hub doesn't bring in members.** It schedules and pays coaches. Members join
   because of marketing, the location, the BFT brand and the coaching. If sign-ups drop,
   you go unpaid for reasons you can't control, while still doing the support work.
2. **"Profit" is easy to make zero.** Rent, salaries, a new studio fit-out or equipment can
   wipe out a month's profit. Subang is in pre-sales, so it will probably run at a loss for a
   while. You'd need to see his accounts every month and agree what counts as profit. That
   is hard to enforce, and it strains a friendly relationship.
3. **The work doesn't stop on a bad month.** Payroll still has to run, and bugs still need
   fixing. "No profit, no pay" means support for free exactly when he needs it most.
4. **He's cost-aware, not cash-poor.** He compared tool prices on the call. He'll value a
   clear, fair, fixed price over an open-ended cut of his business.

**Where a "we win when you win" deal does make sense: Hiiiilo.** Hiiiilo brings in the
leads that become members. A bonus per lead that converts, for example on the Subang
campaign, is tied to something you can measure and affect. Keep that idea for the Hiiiilo
side.

## Three ways to charge for Coach Hub

### Option 1: Fixed price per stage, plus monthly support (simplest)

| Stage | Suggested range |
|---|---|
| Set-up (test copy, backups) + fix the 13 findings | RM 2,500 – 3,500 |
| Finish and test Stage 2 | RM 2,000 – 3,000 |
| Stage 3 pay (rates, invoices, PDF, bank details, bank file) | RM 6,000 – 9,000 |
| Go live + pilot support (2–3 weeks) | RM 1,500 – 2,500 |
| **Total build** | **RM 12,000 – 18,000** |
| Monthly support after launch | RM 500 – 800 / month |

- **Good:** clear, paid as you go, each stage is its own decision for him.
- **Risk:** a bigger number up front for a cost-aware buyer.

### Option 2: Lower build fee + a monthly fee per studio (recommended)

| Part | Suggested range |
|---|---|
| Build (all stages above) | RM 7,000 – 9,000 |
| Monthly fee per active studio (hosting watch, fixes, small changes, payroll-day support) | RM 350 – 500 per studio / month |
| Minimum term | 12 months |

- Today: 2 studios → RM 700 – 1,000/month. With his 4 studios planned by 2027 →
  RM 1,400 – 2,000/month.
- **This is the "partnership" feel you want, done safely:** you earn more as he grows, and
  growth is measured in studios, which anyone can count. No profit maths, no audits.
- **Good:** a smaller first cheque for him, and recurring income for you that grows.
- **Risk:** lower cash up front for you. The 12-month minimum protects that.

### Option 3: Product partnership (bigger upside, later)

BFT has many franchisees, and every one has the same part-time coach problem. After the
pilot proves it works:

- You build Coach Hub for a lower fee, in exchange for the **right to sell it to other BFT
  franchisees** (or other boutique gyms), with Kelvin getting a **revenue share**, e.g.
  10–20% of what other studios pay.
- Kelvin brings the know-how and the first customer. You bring the build, hosting and support.

This is the real long-term partnership, and the one with the biggest upside. **Raise it as
an idea on the call, but don't sign it yet.** It needs a written agreement on who owns the
code and data, and BFT HQ's view on branding (the app uses BFT logos subject to HQ approval).
Get a lawyer to look at it before signing.

### Option 4: "Dedicated IT partner": fixed base + bonus per new member (Aaron's idea, made safe)

One monthly deal covering everything (Coach Hub, Hiiiilo, IT support), with a **floor** so
neither side is ever at zero, and a **bonus** that grows when his studios grow.

| Part | Suggested |
|---|---|
| Coach Hub build | RM 5,000 – 6,000 (heavily reduced) |
| Monthly base (Coach Hub support + Hiiiilo + IT help) | RM 1,500 – 2,000 / month |
| Bonus | RM 20 – 30 per **new paying member** each month, across all studios |
| Term | 12 months, review after 6 |

- New members are counted from his Glofox membership report: a number, not his accounts.
- Bad month: he pays only the base, so his cost is lowest when sales are low.
- Good month: e.g. the Subang launch with 200 sign-ups at RM25 is a RM5,000 bonus.

## Recommendation

1. **Now:** Option 2. Lower build fee, a monthly fee per studio, a 12-month minimum.
2. **On the call:** float Option 3 as a "later" idea: *"If this works well for you, other
   BFT owners have the same problem. We'd love to explore offering it to them together,
   with a share coming back to you."*
3. **On the Hiiiilo side:** if you want performance pay, tie it to converted leads there.

## Always say

- **Running costs are his**, paid directly: Supabase Pro (about US$25/month), Vercel Pro
  (about US$20/month), email and domain. Keep them separate from your fee.
- **Third-party waits are priced separately:** Glofox sync (needs ABC Fitness approval) and
  WhatsApp reminders (needs Meta approval). Their delays aren't yours.
- **Payment terms:** e.g. 50% at the start of each stage, 50% when he signs it off on his phone.
- **Write it down:** a one-page agreement with scope per stage, price, payment terms, who
  owns the code (him, once paid), and what monthly support covers.
